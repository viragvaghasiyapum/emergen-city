-> Important observations about openinframap.org:
  - A third-party service that relies on sources like https://www.gem.wiki/ , https://www.wikidata.org/ , https://commons.wikimedia.org/.
  - Authenticity of some of the data is cross referenced through articles, but authenticity of complete datasets cannot be guaranteed.
  - Datasets are paid.
